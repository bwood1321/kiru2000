The Manager
v8.1 NEW
The only system that learns from results and changes behavior. Everything else follows rules — The Manager watches who's making money and who's losing it, then shifts capital accordingly. Like a boss deciding who gets the budget.

It tracks a rolling window of the last 20 trades per strategy and calculates a trust score (0.25x to 2.0x) that directly multiplies bet size. Hot strategy winning 55%+? Gets up to 2x capital. Cold strategy below 35% win rate? Cut to 0.25x — barely alive but can earn its way back.

Also detects momentum: 3 wins in a row gets a 1.2x bonus. 3 losses in a row gets a 0.6x penalty. Loads history from trade_data.json so it remembers across restarts.
Live Example @ $1,470 balance
LATENCY: trust 1.5x → $88 × 1.5 = $132 🔥
SQUEEZE: trust 1.3x → $44 × 1.3 = $44 (3% cap)
FLASH: trust 0.5x → $44 × 0.5 = $22 ⚠️
MEANREV: trust 0.25x → $44 × 0.25 = $11 ❄️

Trend Engine
v6
Classifies the current BTC market into one of 5 regimes: FLAT, TRENDING_UP, TRENDING_DOWN, CHOPPY, or BREAKOUT. Uses EMA crossovers, RSI, and volatility bands calculated from the live Binance price feed.

Every strategy checks the regime before entering. The hard counter-trend block uses it to prevent buying NO in TRENDING_UP (0% win rate historically). Also provides a trend multiplier that boosts with-trend bets and reduces counter-trend ones.
How it decides
BTC: $68,500 → $68,800 over 3 min
Regime: TRENDING_UP
→ YES trades get 1.2x boost
→ NO trades HARD BLOCKED
⚖️
Adaptive Sizer
v6
Tracks per-strategy performance over longer timeframes and adjusts multipliers between 0.3x and 2.5x. If a strategy loses 5 in a row, it gets paused for 30 minutes. Also tracks which side (YES/NO) is performing better and can cool a side that's bleeding.

Works on a slower timescale than The Manager — thinks in terms of sessions and hours, not individual trades. The Manager handles fast reactions, Adaptive Sizer handles long-term learning.
Difference from The Manager
Manager: "Flash lost 3 in a row → cut to 0.25x NOW"
Sizer: "Flash has 35% WR over 20 trades → mult 0.7x"
Both multiply sizing. Manager is fast. Sizer is slow.
🎯
Conviction Engine
v7
Detects when multiple strategies agree on the same market and direction. If Latency and Flash both want YES on the same market, that's higher conviction — the bet gets a 1.5x boost. Three strategies agreeing gets 1.8x.

This is the only system that looks across strategies. Everything else operates within a single strategy. When the whole bot agrees, it bets bigger.
Example
LATENCY signals YES (BTC just pumped)
FLASH signals YES (token is cheap at $0.15)
→ Conviction: 2 strategies agree → 1.5x bonus
$88 base × 1.5 = $132 bet instead of $88
🚧
Momentum Guard
v7
A blocker, not a booster. Watches if BTC has been moving consistently in one direction for 3+ minutes. If so, it blocks any strategy from betting against that direction — even if the regime engine hasn't classified it as trending yet.

Works with the v8.1 Fast Trend Safety (0.15% in 2 min) as a double layer. Momentum Guard catches slow sustained moves. Fast Trend catches sharp recent ones.
Example
BTC climbing steadily: $68,500 → $68,620 over 4 min
Momentum Guard: "BTC sustained UP for 4 min"
→ Blocks all NO trades regardless of strategy
Even if regime says FLAT (it's lagging)
🔥
Win Streak Sizer
v7
Simple but powerful: when you're on a hot streak, bet more. 3 consecutive wins = 1.2x. 4 wins = 1.3x. 5+ wins = 1.4x. Resets on any loss.

This captures the reality that winning streaks often happen when market conditions are favorable. If you're reading the market right, lean into it. One loss and you're back to normal.
Example
Win → Win → Win → Win
4-streak active → all bets × 1.3x
Next trade: Loss
→ Reset to 1.0x immediately

Token Price Feed
v8
Before v8, the bot only watched Binance BTC price. It was blind to what Polymarket tokens were actually doing. Token Feed tracks YES/NO prices every 10 seconds and calculates:

Velocity — is the token price accelerating or decelerating? Used by MeanReversion to detect when a drop is slowing down (time to buy the bounce).
Divergence — is the token moving differently from BTC? If BTC is flat but YES is climbing, someone knows something.
Smart money — token moving without a BTC cause means hidden buying/selling pressure.
What it sees
YES price: $0.35 → $0.22 → $0.19 → $0.18 → $0.18
Token dropped 49% but DECELERATING (slowing)
→ MeanReversion: "Drop exhausting, buy the bounce"
📚
Order Book Intel
v8
Reads the Polymarket order book every 8-10 seconds. Calculates bid/ask depth (how much money is stacked on each side), imbalance ratio (buyers vs sellers), and detects whales (single orders over $200).

Flash uses it to avoid buying when there's a thick sell wall (asks 2.5x bigger than bids = sellers dominating, price will keep dropping). MeanReversion uses it to confirm that bid support is forming before buying the bounce.
What it sees
YES book: $340 in bids, $890 in asks
Imbalance: 0.38 (sellers dominating)
→ Flash: "Sell wall detected, skip this entry"

YES book: $600 in bids, $200 in asks
Imbalance: 3.0 (buyers stepping in)
→ MeanReversion: "Support forming, safe to buy"
